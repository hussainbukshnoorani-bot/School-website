Our IB Programmes

PYP

Ages 3–12

Primary Years Programme — inquiry-based learning built around big, transdisciplinary themes that spark curiosity.

MYP

Ages 11–16

Middle Years Programme — students connect a broad range of subjects to the real world, building toward a personal project.

DP

Ages 16–19

Diploma Programme — the internationally recognized, university-entrance qualification IB is best known for.

CP

Ages 16–19

Career-related Programme — blends DP courses with career-focused study for a professional pathway.
